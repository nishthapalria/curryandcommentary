Which Makeup Product Is Your Personality, Based on Your Zodiac Sign

Let's be honest, your horoscope doesn't decide if Mercury is in retrograde. Your makeup bag does.

So we matched every zodiac sign to a makeup product based on how you actually act, not what your zodiac sign tells you. Pick your sign, check your vibe, find your makeup twin — that one product in your bag that behaves exactly like you do on a normal day.

Fire Signs: All Drama & Main Character Energy

Aries — Red Liquid Lipstick

Bold, impulsive, first to speak. One swipe and the whole look is DONE. Smudges when angry.

Leo — Highlighter

Literally cannot be ignored. Needs light to hit it at all times. Main character shimmer.

Sagittarius — Setting Spray

Wild, free, keeps everything together while booking a trip. "It's not going anywhere" energy until it does.

Earth Signs: Beat Locked In Place

Taurus — Cream Blush

Soft, rich, expensive-looking, stays all day. Obsessed with looking naturally flushed and well-fed.

Virgo — Concealer

Precise, perfectionist, covers everyone's mistakes. Looks innocent but is judging your blending technique.

Capricorn — Brow Pomade

Structured, sharp, business-like. Not here to play, here to get your brows and life in order.

Air Signs: Made of Glitter & Opinions

Gemini — Eyeshadow Palette

Two moods, 18 shades. Sweet one second, smoky the next. Can do every look and still can't decide.

Libra — Lip Gloss

Balanced, pretty, universally liked. Trying to make everything shiny and harmonious.

Aquarius — Colored Eyeliner / Graphic Liner

Weird in the best way. Doesn't follow trends, starts them. Blue wing? Why not.

Water Signs: Emotional, Waterproof (Hopefully)

Cancer — Mascara

Nostalgic, emotional, will make you cry and then make your lashes look amazing while you do.

Scorpio — Black Kajal / Kohl

Intense, smudgy, mysterious. Unassuming stick but holds all your secrets and vengeance.

Pisces — Dewy Foundation

Soft, dreamy, skin-like. Romanticizes everything and melts if you feel too much.

Are you the soft Lip Oil who mothers everyone, the Graphic Liner who doesn't follow rules, or the Red Lipstick who will absolutely start a fight and win it?
