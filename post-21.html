What Your Bollywood Crush Says About the Life You Secretly Want

Your fictional crush might reveal the kind of life you secretly aspire to.

Whether it's Ranveer Singh from Dil Dhadakne Do, Ranbir Kapoor from Yeh Jawaani Hai Deewani, or Fawad Khan from Khoobsurat, the fictional men we fall for might say more about us than we'd like to admit. So, let's find out what your Bollywood crush says about the life you secretly want. 👀

If you like Ranveer Singh from Dil Dhadakne Do…

Kabir Mehra has an independent career, his own money, and the confidence to make his own choices — and honestly, that's hot.

You don't just want financial independence. You want the kind of money where checking the price tag on your skincare, bags, or that ridiculously expensive vacation becomes a thing of the past.

You aspire to be RICH. Very rich. Like, "Do I want it?" instead of "Can I afford it?" rich. (We all do. 😂)

If you like Ranbir Kapoor from Yeh Jawaani Hai Deewani…

Bunny doesn't just dream about an exciting life. He goes out, meets people, travels, takes risks, and actually works towards the life he wants. And somewhere deep down, you want that too.

Except… you're still sitting at home imagining your dream life instead of actually going out and living it. Girl, GO OUT. You have stories to tell. Places to see. People to meet. A whole life waiting for you.

You aspire to be FAMOUS. And honestly? You might just be meant to be.

If you like Fawad Khan from Khoobsurat…

Like Prince Vikram, you have ambition. You have goals. You have plans. You have things to prove.

But what you really want is someone to look at your 47-item mental to-do list and simply say: "Relax. I've got this."

You don't want to stop being ambitious. You just want life to feel a little less stressful while you're achieving everything. You aspire to be CHILL. Keep going, girl. You're getting there. 😌

If you like Shahid Kapoor from Jab We Met…

Aditya Kashyap is the kind of guy who can look at your absolute chaos and somehow think, "Yep. That's my girl." He doesn't need you to become a completely different person before he loves you. And come on… we all know how rare that is. 😂

You don't want someone constantly telling you how to improve, become better, dress differently, talk differently, or "calm down." You want someone who can look at your madness and say, "I love her exactly like this."

Your fuel is emotion. Your heart runs the show. And honestly? You're a little crazy at heart. You aspire to GET MARRIED. Seriously. 🫠

Now be honest — did this quiz just expose your real-life aspiration? Are you secretly trying to become rich, famous, chill… or are you just trying to get married already? 😂
