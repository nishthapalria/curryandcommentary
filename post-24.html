I Went Out to Make Friends and Came Home Hungry, to My Favourite Person

So I signed up for this random event where you meet people from different countries. Kyuki ghar pade pade bore ho gayi thi.

Toh I booked my Uber, navigated my map, and nikal pade hum dost banane.

I reached there, met people, and I was like... okay Nishtha, finally some exposure. We are growing.

Everything was going fine until the food came.

Now, I am vegetarian. There was this one thing that looked exactly like a cutlet. So I asked someone, "Is this vegetarian?" And they said yes.

Lekin mera dimag, jo faltu baaton mein bhi overthinking kar leta hai, isme kaise na kare. Toh maine ek aur insaan se pucha...

And she said, "This is a lobster cutlet."

And listen, I did not vomit. I simply smiled, said no thank you, and started waiting for the veg options to show up.

Then finally my vegetarian food came. Salad. Bhai, le de ke... SALAD.

Guess what? If you know me, you know — bhukhe pet toh mujhe main khud achi nahi lagti, toh naye dost kya banaungi?

Toh main fatafat bahana bana kar wahan se nikli. Aur mujhe dhyan aaya Superman, aka mere pati ka.

And the second I got into the car, I texted my husband: "Mere pet mein bhookh ke chuhe daud rahe hain."

And my cutu husband, who has clearly understood the assignment, immediately ordered fries.

Aur phir hum dono ne saath mein TV ke saamne fries thooste-thooste poore event ka analysis kiya. Kaun interesting tha. Kaun thoda weird tha. Kisne kya bola. And he laughed so much.

Basically, poori duniya ko dost banane nikli thi... aur raat ko apne pati ke saath baith ke sabki gossip kar rahi thi.

Maybe this is how it starts.

Filhaal toh... banenge ek din naye dost. Unhi ko sunane ke liye kahaniyan ikatthi kar rahi hoon. Tab tak ke liye, mere cutu pati hi mera dost hai.
